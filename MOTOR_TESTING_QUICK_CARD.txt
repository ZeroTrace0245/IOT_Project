## TROUBLESHOOTING IN 30 SECONDS

| Issue | Check | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| No sound | Wires, power | Reseat connections, check COM port |
| Same speed always | PWM, firmware | Try different values (0, 127, 255) |
| Stuttering | Power supply | Reduce load, check voltage |
| Late response | Serial lag | Normal if >100ms, try again |
| No log | ListView issue | Refresh page or restart app |
